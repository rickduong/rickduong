# Photography

A gallery of selected cycling photographs, visual moments, and storytelling images.

## Goals

- highlight the atmosphere and mood of the ride
- collect memorable frames from key days on the bike
- keep the strongest images connected to the ride stories they belong to

## Suggested organization

- by ride
- by season
- by place
- by photo type (landscape, detail, portraits, ride scenes)

## Add a gallery

Use a supported naming pattern like:

```text
YYYY-MM-DD-photo-set.md
```

or a more descriptive title:

```text
ramrod-photos.md
```
